Gym Fresher Tips

Essential knowledge for beginners that most people wish they knew earlier.

🥩

Protein is King

Aim for 1.6–2.2g of protein per kg of bodyweight daily. Chicken, eggs, fish, and lentils are great sources.

💧

Stay Hydrated

Drink at least 3–4 litres of water per day. Dehydration reduces strength by up to 10%.

😴

Sleep = Growth

Muscles grow during sleep, not during workouts. Get 7–9 hours every night for maximum gains.

📈

Progressive Overload

Add 2.5kg every 1–2 weeks to your main lifts. Small increases compound into massive strength gains.

🧘

Warm Up First

Spend 5–10 minutes warming up with light cardio and dynamic stretches to prevent injury.

🍽️

Pre/Post Workout Meals

Eat carbs + protein 1–2h before training. Have protein within 30–60min after for best recovery.

📋

Track Everything

Use the tracker above to log your lifts. Seeing progress on paper is the biggest motivation booster.

🎯

Focus on Form First

Never sacrifice form for weight. Perfect technique with lighter weights builds more muscle and prevents injury.

🔁

Stay Consistent

Show up even on bad days. Consistency over 3–6 months beats any "perfect" program that you quit after 2 weeks.
